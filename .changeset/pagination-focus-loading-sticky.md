KunPagination keeps keyboard focus while a page loads, and KunLoading's overlay indicator stays in view over a list taller than the viewport.

- **KunPagination**: while `isLoading` is set, and at the first or last page, the page buttons, prev / next and the jump button are now `aria-disabled="true"` rather than natively `disabled`, and the jump field is `readonly` instead of `disabled`. Clicks, Enter and the arrow keys are still ignored while loading. The reason is Firefox: when a focused control becomes natively disabled, Firefox 155 moves focus to `<body>`, so a keyboard or screen-reader user who pressed "2" lost their place as soon as the load started. Chrome 153 keeps focus either way. The change follows eBay's pagination accessibility guidance: the page and prev / next controls stay focusable at all times, even when visually disabled, and focus stays on the activated control during client-side paging. The visuals are unchanged. If your CSS targets `:disabled` on these controls, switch it to `[aria-disabled="true"]`.
- **KunButton** gains `focusableWhenDisabled` (default `false`), the same idea as Base UI's prop of that name. When it is set, a `disabled` or `loading` button is marked `aria-disabled="true"` instead of the native `disabled`, so it keeps focus, and its click handler still blocks the click. A `disabled` or `loading` button in link mode (`href`) now also carries `aria-disabled="true"`. It was previously announced as a working link.
- **KunLoading** in wrapper mode: if the wrapped box is at least as tall as the viewport, the image or spinner and the text now stick near the middle of the scrollport instead of the middle of the box, and they never leave the box. Before this change, starting a load from the bottom of a long list (for example by clicking the pagination under it) put the indicator far above the viewport. A shorter box keeps the box-centred indicator exactly as before. The overlay is now a size container named `kun-loading`. The limit: an ancestor with `overflow: hidden`, `auto` or `scroll` becomes the scrollport, and the indicator centres on that ancestor instead of the viewport. `overflow: clip` does not have this effect.

KunPagination still does not scroll the page when the page changes. The component cannot know where the list starts, when the new data has rendered, how tall a sticky header is, or whether this particular change should jump at all. Under Nuxt, a `page-href` navigation that only changes the query (`?page=2`) also keeps the scroll position by default. The pagination docs now include a "列表内换页" (in-place paging) section with the recommended recipe. Once the new page has rendered, and only if the list's heading has scrolled out of view, move focus to that heading (`tabindex="-1"`, `focus({ preventScroll: true })`), then call `scrollIntoView`. Use `scroll-margin-top` to clear the sticky header, and skip smooth scrolling under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
